Privacy Notice

The Department of Biodiversity, Conservation and Attractions (DBCA) collects this personal information to assess, approve and grant an Apiary Authority that allows you to legally carry out beekeeping activities in Western Australia's national parks, conservation reserves and certain Crown lands for beekeeping.

You are required to provide this information under the Conservation and Land Management Act 1984 and Conservation and Land Management Regulations 2002.

If you choose not to provide personal information, you will not be able to legally carry out beekeeping activities in Western Australia's national parks, conservation reserves and certain Crown lands for beekeeping.

For further details on how DBCA manage your personal information, you can read our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions about how your personal information will be handled, or if you would like to access your personal information, please email privacy@dbca.wa.gov.au.
